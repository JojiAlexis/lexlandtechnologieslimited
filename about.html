About Us

A Team Built for Digital Growth

Lexland Technologies Limited is a modern technology company founded by JOJI ALEXIS AONDONA and his dedicated team members. We are dedicated to helping businesses build a strong digital presence through creativity, innovation, and reliable technology solutions. The company was founded in 2022 and incorporated with the Corporate Affairs Commission in 2026.

We provide reliable web development, software solutions, graphics design, Google Business Profile and Optimisation, branding, cyber security architecture, and digital support services tailored to meet modern business needs.

At Lexland Technologies Limited, we are proud to deliver world-class technology solutions to clients across the globe. Our services are provided worldwide and internationally, transcending borders to serve businesses in diverse markets and regions. Whether you are a startup in Europe, Asia, Australia, an enterprise in North America, or a growing company in Asia, we bring reliable, innovative, and scalable technology solutions tailored to your specific needs.

Our global delivery model ensures seamless project execution, 24/7 support, and consistent quality regardless of time zone or location. With a strong international presence and a commitment to excellence, Lexland Technologies Limited is your trusted technology partner on the global stage, connecting innovation with opportunity worldwide.
